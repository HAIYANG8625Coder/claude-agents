# Listener profile

The editor reads this before every edition. Keep it short and concrete; edit it whenever an episode misses.

## Background
- Lives and works in Singapore.
- Posts about Singapore life on Xiaohongshu (for Chinese readers), so a clear, practical rule change or money story makes a good post.
- Learning English: listens in English and reads the Chinese notes for terms. Clear spoken English; explain local acronyms once.
- Listens on the phone in the morning, before the separate AI episode.

## Main focus (most of every episode)
1. **Immigration:** Employment Pass, S Pass, COMPASS points, dependant passes, PR and citizenship, visas, ICA and MOM rule changes, fees and salary thresholds, numbers of new PRs and citizens.
2. **Job market:** hiring and layoffs, salaries, which industries are growing or cutting, MOM labour market reports, unemployment, SkillsFuture and career schemes, tech and finance jobs.
3. **Finance:** SGX and big local companies (DBS, OCBC, UOB, Singtel, REITs), interest rates (SORA, T-bills, SSB), the economy and GDP, property prices and rents, CPF, tax, personal money.

## A little of (one story a day)
- **Entertainment:** local shows and celebrities, Mediacorp, concerts and big events in Singapore, what big local creators are doing.

## Does not want
- Ceremonies, ministerial visits and speeches with no new measure.
- Food reviews and promotions.
- Crime and accident stories unless they lead to a rule change or a public warning (e.g. a new scam).
- Buy or sell advice; taking a political side.
