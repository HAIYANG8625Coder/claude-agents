# My X voice (template)

Copy this file to `voice.md` and fill it in. `voice.md` is git-ignored so your personal details stay on your machine. Both agents (x-drafter, x-reviewer) read it before every post.

## Who I am
- Account: @your_handle
- What you do (e.g. "programmer in <city>")
- What you're learning / building right now
- Interests: topic 1, topic 2, topic 3

## How I sound
- e.g. Friendly, simple, honest. Short sentences.
- e.g. Shares what he's learning, not an expert or a brand.
- Emoji: 0–1 per post.

## Avoid
- Hype words: "game-changer", "revolutionary", "excited to announce", "delve".
- Pretending to know more than I do.
- Anything private: email, phone, address, employer.
- Topics you never want in posts (family, politics, ...).

## Example posts I like
- Paste 3–5 real posts that sound the way you want to sound.
