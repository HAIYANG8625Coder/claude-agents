# News & analysis playbook (learned from 小Lin说 on Xiaohongshu)

Studied 2026-11-13: 小Lin说 (rednote ID 636461303, 2.3M followers, 5.5M likes and saves; 北大 → 哥大 → JPMorgan; tagline "商业财经不无聊"). Watched frames of "日元创40年新低，美国突然出手，怎么回事儿" (7.4K likes, 1.8K saves) and "Deepseek突然连融两轮500亿，梁文锋到底想…" (20.8K likes, 6:26 long). Read titles of her last ~30 notes. Use this for any news, company or market video.

## Titles (the cover question)
- An event plus the viewer's own question: "X，怎么回事儿" / "X，到底想干什么" / "X 现在怎么样了？" / "为什么打仗了黄金不涨".
- One surprise number or superlative in the title: "40年新低", "500亿", "史上最大", "一年狂揽$XX亿".
- A word in quotes for the twist: "阳谋", "免税", "拉".
- Her best-liked titles (31–37K) are questions ordinary people already ask: "你需要多少钱，才能财富自由？", "王健林现在怎么样了？"

## Story structure (her news videos, compressed for our 40–60 s)
1. **The event in one line with its biggest number** ("一度摸到了164关口"), on a real chart or real headline.
2. **"原因变了" / the real reason.** What people assume vs what's actually happening ("往年是利差，今年这个规律失效了").
3. **表面 vs 实际 (motive).** "表面上是…，实际上最怕的是…". She always shows who wants what.
4. **The dilemma / who pays.** "加息拖经济，不加息日元失血". Make the trade-off concrete.
5. **An analogy to something familiar.** "这个剧本和2022年英国特拉斯很像", "本质是央行版的抵押贷款". One per video, from real history or everyday life.
6. **Outcome + her view.** What happened next, then a short opinion clearly framed as hers ("我觉得…").
Our short version: 1 → 2 → 5 → 4 → takeaway/question. Skip 3 if there's no real motive story; never invent one.

## Voice
- Talks to a friend: "你看看这个图片多清晰", "他故意给媒体看的", "最近动静小多了", "说白了".
- Every term gets a one-line plain explanation the moment it appears (her AGI note explains "通用人工智能" as "AI界的全能选手").
- Contrast words carry the story: "但是", "反而", "其实", "真正的原因是".
- Calm and curious, never alarmist. Facts first, opinion last and labeled as opinion.

## Visuals (what's on screen)
- **Real evidence every few seconds:** real price charts with glowing dots and big callout numbers at the key points; real news photos; real headlines and article pages with the key phrase **highlighted in pink/yellow marker**; real documents zoomed in ("Buy Japanese Yen" note).
- **Collage of headlines** to show "everyone is talking about it": several real news cards stacked at angles.
- Her face as a small **circle inset** over B-roll, or a split screen (face left, evidence right) when she points at something.
- Clean white captions with a soft shadow, bottom-centre, short phrases.
- Fast cuts: a new image every 2–5 s.
For us (no face yet): use the same evidence-first approach. Paraphrased headline cards with a marker highlight, real numbers as glowing chart callouts, labeled "意译 · 来源 X". Never copy a real outlet's page, logo or exact headline.

## Lessons from using it
- 2026-11-13 (Microsoft outage): the scripter wrote "原因变了 / 微软改口" when Microsoft had only *clarified* (elevated load → caused by servers dropping out, not traffic). The reviewer caught it. Don't force a reversal: use "说法更新了 / 后来补充" unless the source really contradicts itself. Also add "根本原因还没公布" when it isn't.
- Our clone's real rate is ~4.6 chars/s raw, ~4.2 after the 0.92 slowdown. Count English names as 2 chars each.

## What not to copy
- Her length (6–10 min, landscape). Ours stays vertical and 40–60 s.
- Brand deals and lottery posts.
